Frequently Asked Questions

Booking & Session Details

How far in advance should we reserve our wedding date?

Due to high demand for peak wedding seasons in Egypt (Spring and Autumn), we recommend booking 3 to 6 months in advance to ensure our lead team's availability.

When and how do we receive our final edited gallery?

You will receive a curated sneak peek gallery within 48 to 72 hours following your session. Full high-resolution color-graded collections and private cloud access are typically delivered within 3 to 4 weeks.

Do you travel outside Cairo for destination weddings?

Yes! We regularly travel across Alexandria, El Gouna, Sharm El Sheikh, Luxor, Aswan, Upper Egypt (Minya), and international destinations.
